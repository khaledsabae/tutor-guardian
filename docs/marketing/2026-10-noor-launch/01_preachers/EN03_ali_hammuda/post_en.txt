🌙 Al-Morabbi: an Islamic parenting companion for families, from pregnancy to 18, in English and Arabic.
Done for the sake of Allah: 100% free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and nothing asked in return.

Ramadan is coming. The app's Family Ramadan program gives you thirty days, with one family challenge a day of fifteen minutes or less, and a role for every child by age:
• The intentions board: parents share their intention first, then the oldest child down to the youngest.
• The charity box: decorate it together; everyone puts in something, however little.
• A plate for the neighbour, carried by the children with a parent and a kind word.
• The gratitude jar: three small blessings from each person's day.
• A gentle fasting ladder: no fasting before seven in this program, and breaking the fast when tired is not failure.
• Our Ramadan harvest: each person names one thing they achieved and one thing that changed in them.

Also in the app:
• A daily path of three small steps that parents understand and can try with their children.
• An assistant that answers in English, with one practical step for your child's age.
• Bedtime stories narrated by Noor, with a pause to talk together.
• Qur'an and hadith are always quoted in Arabic, with the meaning explained alongside.
