May Allah bless your work, and the families who benefit from your talks on raising children.

We are working on Al-Morabbi, an Islamic parenting companion for families from pregnancy to age 18, in English and Arabic.
Parents in the West often know what they want for their children. What they lack is a small, doable step for today. So the app gives them one step a day and an assistant that answers in English. For Ramadan it has a Family Ramadan program: thirty days, one family challenge a day of fifteen minutes or less, and a role for every child by age.

It is done for the sake of Allah: 100% free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and nothing asked in return.

Ahead of Ramadan, we prepared something from the program itself: an infographic, "Family Ramadan", with a short bullet-point post and screenshots in English.

One small ask: if you find it beneficial, a single post before Ramadan would be enough, shared as it is.
And there is no pressure at all if it isn't possible.
JazakAllahu khayran.
