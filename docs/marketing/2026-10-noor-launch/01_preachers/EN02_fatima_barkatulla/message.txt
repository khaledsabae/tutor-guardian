May Allah bless your work with Raising Believers and the families it reaches.

We are working on Al-Morabbi, an Islamic parenting companion for families from pregnancy to age 18, in English and Arabic.
Parents raising children in the West face the same things again and again. Their children ask about Allah in classrooms where few others believe. They need an answer tonight, not after a course. And screens are everywhere.
So the app gives parents one small step a day, and an assistant that answers in English with a step for their child's age. There is also a child's corner: a short story, then a task away from the screen, and only the parents' PIN unlocks the device again.

It is done for the sake of Allah: 100% free, with no ads, no subscriptions, and nothing asked in return.

We prepared something from the app's own content for your audience: an infographic, "When your child asks about Allah", with a short bullet-point post and screenshots in English.

One small ask: if you find it beneficial, a single post would be enough, shared as it is.
And there is no pressure at all if it isn't possible.
JazakAllahu khayran.
