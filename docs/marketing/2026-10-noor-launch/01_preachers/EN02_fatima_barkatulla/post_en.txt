When your child asks about Allah, here are a few steps from the app's "Foundations of Aqeedah" path:
• Start with love: Allah is Generous and Merciful and loves us. Say this before any talk of punishment.
• On walks, ask: "Who created this?" about a flower, a star or a butterfly.
• If they ask "Who created Allah?", listen calmly. One question says nothing bad about their faith.
• When you don't know the answer, don't guess. Write the question down together and ask people of knowledge.
• Their worth with Allah is in their deeds, not their clothes, their friends or their grades.
