We are working on Al-Morabbi, an Islamic parenting companion for families from pregnancy to age 18.
Each day it gives parents one small step they understand and can try. An assistant answers their questions, and there are bedtime stories for the children.
The app is available in English as well as Arabic: the interface, the curriculum, the stories and the assistant. Qur'an and hadith stay in Arabic, with the meaning explained alongside.

It is a project done for the sake of Allah: 100% free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

We think it may serve your audience, especially Muslim families living in the West. One of its paths helps parents answer their children's questions about Allah and their faith, and build a confident Muslim identity wherever they live.

If you see benefit in it, we have prepared a ready-to-post summary in bullet points, in English and Arabic. It comes with an infographic built from the app's own content, "When your child asks about Allah", and screenshots in both languages, so it can be shared as it is.

And there is no pressure at all if it isn't possible.
JazakAllahu khayran.
