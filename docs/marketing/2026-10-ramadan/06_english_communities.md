## 1. Three honest constraints before anything else

1. **Android only.** StatCounter (mobile web, volatile month to month) puts iOS at roughly half of US and UK mobile traffic.
   Every English post says "Android only for now". Anything else spends goodwill on people who cannot install.
2. **Not everything is in English yet.** Lessons, paths and the assistant are fully English. The adhkar, the daily reminder,
   the games and the story library are **Arabic only** (⏳ F-EN-CONTENT). Say so if asked — the Play listing already does.
3. **No English landing page.** Every web page (`/go`, `/l/…`) is Arabic, so English links go straight to Play with a
   referral code: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alsaba.almorabbi&referrer=ref_CM01&hl=en`.
   This counts installs per code but not clicks (README §3).

## 3. A respectful way to post

1. **Participate before you post.** From mid-October (timeline week 3), Khaled reads and answers genuinely in the
   communities he plans to use, without links. A rough guide used across Reddit: at least nine parts real participation
   to one part anything about your own work.
2. **Read the rules and pinned posts on the day,** then **ask the admin or moderators first**. A no is final for the season.
3. **One post per community per season,** in the window **18 January – 7 February**.
4. **Value first.** The post must be useful even to someone who never installs: a full, concrete tip in the body; the link last.
5. **Disclose every time:** "I built this. It's free, no ads, nothing to sell."
6. **Never** DM members, use a second account, write as a "parent who found this app", or post reviews or testimonials.
7. **No religious debates and no rulings** in the comments. Questions of halal and haram: "please ask a scholar you trust".
8. **Answer every comment within 24 hours** for the first three days. If the post is removed, do not repost.
9. **Say the limits before anyone asks:** Android only; some content Arabic-only.

## 4. Templates

### Group post (only after the admin says yes) ⏳ F-RAMADAN

```
Salaam everyone — with the admins' permission.

One idea for a first fast at 7–9: start with half a day. Let your child fast until Dhuhr, break
the fast together, and ask: "What did you feel when you were hungry?" For older children, agree on
a fasting goal together and celebrate when they reach it. The aim this year is that they understand
fasting and love it — not the number of hours.

Full disclosure: I built a free app for Muslim parents called Al-Morabbi. This Ramadan it has a
30-day family program — one small challenge a day and fasting practice matched to each child's age.
No ads, nothing to buy. Android only for now, and a few parts (adhkar, games, stories) are still Arabic-only.
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alsaba.almorabbi&referrer=ref_[CODE]&hl=en

Happy to hear what would make it more useful.
```
