🌙 Al-Morabbi: an Islamic parenting companion for families, from pregnancy to 18, in English and Arabic.
Done for the sake of Allah: 100% free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

Ramadan is coming. The app's Family Ramadan program gives you thirty days, with one family challenge a day of fifteen minutes or less, and a role for every child by age.

From the program, for the last ten nights:
• The family plan: Prayer, du'a and Quran. Little ones join early and sleep on time; older ones carry on if they wish.
• The first night: Two short rak'ahs at home; the child chooses his place in the row beside a parent.
• The du'a wall: Du'as for yourselves, your family and people in need; parents write for the little ones.
• A night of listening: Bright lights off, phones away, and a calm recitation of a short surah you know.
• A night of hope: Everyone writes a hope for the coming year and puts it in the Ramadan corner.
• Our Ramadan harvest: Choose two Ramadan habits to keep after Eid, on a “family promise card”.

Also in the app:
• A Ramadan lantern street, lit by what you do together, day by day.
• A daily path of three small steps that parents can try with their children.
• Bedtime stories narrated by Noor, with a pause to talk together.
• An assistant that answers in English, with one practical step for your child's age.
