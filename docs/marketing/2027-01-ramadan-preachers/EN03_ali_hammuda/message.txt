May Allah bless your work, and the families who benefit from your talks on raising children.

Ramadan is coming, and this is what we have prepared for your families.

Al-Morabbi is still done for the sake of Allah: 100% free, with no ads and no subscriptions. For Ramadan it has a Family Ramadan program: thirty days, one family challenge a day of fifteen minutes or less, and a role for every child by age.

Since the last ten nights are where families most want to bring their children along, we prepared "The last ten nights with your children" from the program itself: an infographic, a short bullet-point post and screenshots in English, ready to share as they are if you find them beneficial.

The best time would be about two weeks before Ramadan, so that families arrive as the program begins.
And there is no pressure at all if it isn't possible.
JazakAllahu khayran.
