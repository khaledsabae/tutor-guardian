🌙 Al-Morabbi: an Islamic parenting companion for families, from pregnancy to 18, in English and Arabic.
Done for the sake of Allah: 100% free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

Ramadan is coming. The app's Family Ramadan program gives you thirty days, with one family challenge a day of fifteen minutes or less, and a role for every child by age.

From the program's fasting ladder, for children before puberty:
• Training, not obligation: Before puberty, fasting is training. The aim is for the child to love fasting.
• No fasting before seven: Little ones share the atmosphere of Ramadan without fasting.
• Ages 4–6: waiting for the adhan: He saves a pitted date until maghrib to eat with the family; his meals stay complete.
• Ages 7–9: hours, not days: About three hours after suhoor at first, building up gradually, rest days first.
• One step at a time: Move up only after two or three comfortable days, and only if the child wants to.
• Breaking the fast is not failure: “Well done for trying; tomorrow is a new day.” No blame, no comparing siblings.

Also in the app:
• A Ramadan lantern street, lit by what you do together, day by day.
• A daily path of three small steps that parents can try with their children.
• Bedtime stories narrated by Noor, with a pause to talk together.
• An assistant that answers in English, with one practical step for your child's age.
