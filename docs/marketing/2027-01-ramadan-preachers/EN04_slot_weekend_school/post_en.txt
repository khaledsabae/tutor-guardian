🌙 Al-Morabbi: an Islamic parenting companion for families, from pregnancy to 18, in English and Arabic.
Done for the sake of Allah: 100% free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

Ramadan is coming. The app's Family Ramadan program gives you thirty days, with one family challenge a day, and a role for every child by age.

From the program, for children aged seven to nine:
• Ramadan corner designer: Draws the crescents and stars, and makes a chart with thirty boxes to tick off each day.
• A job at the table: Water and cups before the adhan, and he starts his food with “Bismillah”.
• Fasting by the hour: About three hours after suhoor, then until dhuhr after a comfortable week.
• Three kind words: Says them to his family today; if he gets angry, he steps away until he calms down.
• From his pocket money: Puts part of it in the family charity box, even a little.
• The seed notebook: Draws the seeds every day, and writes something he was patient about.

Also in the app:
• A Ramadan lantern street, lit by what you do together, day by day.
• A daily path of three small steps that parents can try with their children.
• Bedtime stories narrated by Noor, with a pause to talk together.
• An assistant that answers in English, with one practical step for your child's age.
