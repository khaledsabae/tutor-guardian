🌙 Al-Morabbi: an Islamic parenting companion for families, from pregnancy to 18, in English and Arabic.
Done for the sake of Allah: 100% free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

Ramadan is coming. The app's Family Ramadan program gives you thirty days, with one family challenge a day of fifteen minutes or less, and a role for every child by age.

From the program's fasting ladder:
• Doctor first: With diabetes or any chronic illness, or a medicine taken regularly during the day.
• Also: Being very underweight or slow growth, or a recent illness such as fever, diarrhoea or vomiting.
• Stop signs: Dizziness, paleness or a cold sweat; a severe headache; vomiting; very little or very dark urine.
• When a sign appears: He breaks the fast at once with water and a date or juice, then rests. No blame, no comparison.
• Suhoor: Water plus slow-digesting food: wholemeal bread, oats, eggs, yoghurt. Not sweets on their own.
• Water little and often: From iftar to bedtime, not a large amount all at once.

Also in the app:
• A Ramadan lantern street, lit by what you do together, day by day.
• A daily path of three small steps that parents can try with their children.
• Bedtime stories narrated by Noor, with a pause to talk together.
• An assistant that answers in English, with one practical step for your child's age.
