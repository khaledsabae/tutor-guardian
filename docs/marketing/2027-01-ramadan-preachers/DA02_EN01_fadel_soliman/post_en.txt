🌙 Al-Morabbi: an Islamic parenting companion for families, from pregnancy to 18, in English and Arabic.
Done for the sake of Allah: 100% free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

Ramadan is coming. The app's Family Ramadan program gives you thirty days, with one family challenge a day, and a role for every child by age.

Some of the program's challenges, seen by neighbours, relatives and those who serve us:
• The fasting tongue: Whoever is provoked says the family's agreed calm phrase, then walks away.
• A plate for the neighbour: The children carry it with a parent, with a kind word and Ramadan greetings.
• Clearing harm from the way: Fifteen minutes to tidy a shared place: the entrance or the stairs.
• Keeping family ties: Call a relative you haven't spoken to for a while; the children greet them.
• Mercy to all creatures: A small dish of water and a few grains for the birds, refreshed every day.
• Thanking those who serve us: Cards for the building guard, the cleaner, a teacher or a nurse.

Also in the app:
• A Ramadan lantern street, lit by what you do together, day by day.
• A daily path of three small steps that parents can try with their children.
• Bedtime stories narrated by Noor, with a pause to talk together.
• An assistant that answers in English, with one practical step for your child's age.
