## Lesson Summary
The Prophetic tradition meticulously outlines spiritual and emotional milestones for welcoming a newborn: reciting the Adhan in the right ear so Tawheed is the very first message heard, gentle tahneek with a softened date, selecting an honorable name, offering the Aqeeqah on the seventh day to celebrate and share blessings, shaving the head and giving silver in charity, and reciting daily protective prophetic prayers (Ruqyah).

## Try This This Week
This week: Prepare a list of authentic protective supplications (e.g., 'I seek refuge for you in the perfect words of Allah from every devil, poisonous pest, and evil eye') and make reciting them softly a peaceful daily routine during feeding or bedtime.
